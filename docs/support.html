Help & contact

How to play

Pick who you're with. Drag the ring down the slider to choose how deep the questions go, from Shallows to The deep end. Take turns asking; tap Next question to pass the phone. Skip draws a different question, Save keeps one for later, and Note tells us what you think of it.

Common questions

I've run out of questions at a depth. Tap Start over at this depth, or slide to another depth.

Can I get every question back? Menu → Forget which questions we've seen.

Does it work offline? Yes, once it's installed.

Where does my data go? Nowhere. See the privacy policy.
